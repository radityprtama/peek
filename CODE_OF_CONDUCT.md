# Code of Conduct

Peek welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
We want its issues, discussions, pull requests, and other project spaces to be
useful and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent, explain disagreements with
specific reasons, and make room for people to ask questions and learn. Keep
feedback focused on the work and its effects.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized comments or
images, and publication of someone else's private information are not allowed.
Repeatedly disrupting project discussions is also not allowed.

## Reporting and response

Send private conduct reports to **hi@pratama.dev**. Include links or screenshots
and any context needed to understand the concern. Do not post sensitive reports
in public issues or discussions. You can also use GitHub's report function on
the content involved.

The maintainer will review reports, keep details limited to people who need to
know, and may remove content, request changes in behavior, or restrict access
to project spaces. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is not
allowed. This policy applies to all project spaces and to public conduct that
directly affects participation in Peek.
